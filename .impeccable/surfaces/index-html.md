## Audience / job
Hackathon judges and recruiters: understand who Mahantesh is, find About / Superpowers / Projects instantly, discover and play Rewind.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a machine spec sheet of one engineer, and Rewind re-boots it on the hardware and web of 2012, 2016, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2026. Refuses the dark-hero "Hi, I'm X" dev portfolio with skill-icon grids.

OWN-WORLD (2026 default): component datasheet. White sheet, ink #111, hairline rules, spec tables with tabular figures, Archivo (wide display, condensed labels) + JetBrains Mono only for measurements, one signal green (#0a7d3b) for "OK/active", one amber for warnings. Era skins are period-faithful re-renderings: 2012 skeuomorphic glossy, 2016 flat Material, 2020 dark glass, 2022 neo-brutalist, 2024 AI-chat era.

STORY: A 14-year data engineer; hardware went from 1 GB iPads to 80 GB GPUs; in 2024 he sat down as a student again. Visitor understands the pivot, trusts real numbers, presses Rewind.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7 cols: name set huge in wide Archivo, part line "Lead AI Engineer · M.Tech AI student, IIT Jodhpur", one-sentence story. Right 5 cols: "Machine" spec block for 2026 (role, memory, models) with the REWIND control as a year switch strip directly under it, pulsing hint "Rewind to 2012 ◀◀". Top nav: About · Superpowers · Projects · Journey · Contact.

FORM: Hardware datasheet + era time machine, user-pinned (overrides roll; original seed key 45703fd3).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
